Method

Three layers.
Never collapsed.

A founding statement, historical behavior, and current implementation behavior are different kinds of evidence. ORIGIN preserves that distinction.

01 / Founding

What was proposed?

Source passages and explicit interpretation boundaries. Architectural claims are not disguised as binary tests.

02 / Historical

What occurred?

Transactions, headers, scripts, releases, and independently reproducible calculations.

03 / Current

What runs now?

Exact revisions, node observations, conformance vectors, and policy fingerprints.
