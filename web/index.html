Methodology

Separate the layers.
Then test the claims.

ORIGIN avoids a single ideological score. Every observation is assigned to the layer it can actually support.

Founding

What was claimed?

Source passages, publication context, and explicit interpretation boundaries.

Historical

What occurred?

Transactions, block headers, scripts, release artifacts, and reproducible calculations.

Current

What runs now?

Exact revisions, local RPC observations, conformance vectors, and policy fingerprints.

Provenance

Who signed what?

Key control, release signatures, source-to-binary consistency, and independent replication.
