# Neutrality Policy

ORIGIN is neutral about names, not neutral about evidence quality.

## What ORIGIN may say

ORIGIN may report that an implementation accepted or rejected a vector, changed between pinned revisions, exposed a local control, reproduced or failed to reproduce an artifact, matched or diverged from a cited observation, or supplied, omitted, or contradicted evidence.

## What ORIGIN does not say

ORIGIN does not declare which implementation is the one true Bitcoin, which person is Satoshi based only on key control, that a keyword proves a backdoor, that protocol seizure resistance eliminates ecosystem coercion, or that popularity validates protocol identity.

## Equal methodology

The same evidence requirements, uncertainty labels, correction process, and review standards apply to every implementation.

## Visible disagreement

When reasonable interpretations differ, reports preserve the evidence and expose competing interpretations rather than forcing a single conclusion.
